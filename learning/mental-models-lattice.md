# Mental Models Lattice

## Overview

The claim behind the lattice is a claim about **judgement**, not about knowledge: the person who knows
many models from many disciplines recognises more situations correctly, because they can see the structure
where a single-discipline expert sees a familiar case.

The framing (attributed in the source to Munger's "latticework") has three parts:

1. Breadth of models beats depth of one model for *decision quality*.
2. Models must come from genuinely different disciplines to decorrelate errors.
3. Models are only useful when you can say **when they fail**.

## The accumulation discipline

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Rate | one new model per week — small enough to sustain, large enough to matter over a year |
| Depth of field | 2–3 core domains deep, the rest sampled for models (avoid "a mile wide, an inch deep") |
| Record | a card with: the model, one worked example, **its failure conditions**, and links to related models |
| Apply | each new model must be used on a live decision within the week; otherwise it is trivia |
| Review | models enter the review rhythm — retrieval, not re-reading ([[review-and-recall-rhythm\|Review and Recall Rhythm]]) |

A year of weekly cards is ~50 models; two years approaches the 80–100 range the heuristic names. The
compounding is not in the count but in the *links*: a model becomes useful when it collides with another.

## The catalogue is already a lattice

This vault's sibling collections show what the cards look like in practice — psychology (dual-process
theory, prospect theory), economics (comparative advantage, transaction costs, monetarism), strategy
(disruptive innovation, Porter's five forces, blue-ocean), systems (antifragility and barbell, flywheel
effects), and diffusion (Rogers' adoption curve). Each card earns its place by being *usable in a decision*
and by declaring where it breaks.

## Model types worth prioritising

- **Bias and calibration models** — dual-process theory, prospect theory, base rates (they govern every
  other judgement).
- **Structural models** — comparative advantage, transaction costs, network effects, diffusion (they
  explain why a plan that "should" work does not).
- **Risk models** — antifragility, barbell strategy, margin of safety (they govern survival, which
  dominates everything else).
- **Decision-process models** — 10-10-10, pre-mortems, ACH ([[investigation-before-judgement|Investigation
  Before Judgement]]).

## Numbers and their status

| Claim | Value | Status |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Cross-disciplinary models to collect | 80–90 | Munger's heuristic, not a measurement |
| Cadence | 1 per week | design choice |
| Core domains to go deep in | 2–3 | design choice |
| Deep reading per day | 1–2 h | habit target from the source |
| "7 years of 1–2 h/day reaches the top of a field" | — | ⚠️ unsupported in the source; motivational, not measurable |

## Anti-patterns

- **Collector mode** — cards accumulate, decisions do not change.
- **Correlated models** — ten models from the same discipline; errors do not cancel.
- **Model absolutism** — using a model because you know it, not because it fits.
- **No failure conditions** — a model without stated limits is a slogan.

## 中文速览

- **判断力来自跨学科模型的网格**，不是单一学科的深度：模型越多、来源越分散，误判越不容易同源。
- **六条积累纪律**：每周 1 个模型 · 2-3 个核心领域深挖（拒绝「一英里宽、一英寸深」）· 每张卡写清「模型 + 一个实例 + **失效条件** + 关联模型」· 每张卡一周内必须用在一个真实决策上 · 模型进入检索式复习 · 靠链接产生复利。
- **优先四类**：偏差与校准（双系统/前景理论/基础率）· 结构类（比较优势/交易成本/网络效应/创新扩散）· 风险类（反脆弱/杠铃/安全边际）· 决策流程类（10-10-10/事前验尸/ACH）。
- ⚠️ 「80-90 个模型」是芒格的启发式而非测量；「每天 1-2 小时读 7 年可达顶尖」源材料无支撑，当激励不当指标。
- 反面模式：只收藏不应用、模型全来自同一学科、因为熟悉而用某个模型、卡片不写失效条件。
