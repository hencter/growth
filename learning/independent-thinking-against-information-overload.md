# Independent Thinking Against Information Overload

## Overview

Overload does not merely waste time; it attenuates the ability to form a position, because the volume
of commentary substitutes for having a view. The counter-measure in the source is not "read less" or
"meditate" but a fixed **ordering**: answer internally first, then take in outside views, then
synthesise something actionable. Ordering is the mechanism — it prevents the flood from pre-writing
your conclusion.

## The four-step frame

1. **Pose the question** — and make it yours.
2. **Decompose it** — three layers (below).
3. **Think with multiple inputs** — deliberately including the ones that disagree.
4. **Summarise into a method** that fits your own situation, not a general truth.

## Make the question open

| Closed (paralysing) | Open (workable) |
|---------------------|-----------------|
| "Is `996` a blessing?" | "What is `996`, how did it form, and how do I, as someone inside it, read it?" |
| "Is X right?" | "Why does X say it this way, and what would change my mind?" |

A closed question has two exits and no learning. An open question has structure to explore.

## Decompose in three layers

| Layer | Questions to answer |
|-------|---------------------|
| **The thing itself** | Definition, where it appears, how it connects to economy/society |
| **The other side's motive** | Their background and values; what they want to achieve; the business logic |
| **My position** | What it does to me; what I can change; what I should do next |

Skipping layer 2 is the most common error: it produces arguments against a motive you never identified.

## Self-answer before outside views

Write your own answer first — as objectively as you can — *then* read others, deliberately prioritising
sources that differ from you. This is the ordering that makes the reading an act of comparison rather
than of ingestion. Long-form work (books, serious periodicals) tends to carry more depth than feeds;
and choose **by author** — the source's heuristic is to prefer authors who are scientist and
philosopher at once (Descartes' *Discourse on the Method*, Aristotle's *Nicomachean Ethics*).

## The anxiety loop to watch for

```
comfortable state → anxiety provoked (e.g. a "get ahead fast" course)
        → seeking a solution → purchase decision
```

This loop sells the cure for the anxiety it manufactured. Naming the loop is usually enough to exit it;
the exit is a question of your own, not a product.

## 中文速览

- **核心机制是顺序，不是数量**：先写自己的回答 → 再听外部声音（尤其听不一样的）→ 最后提炼成符合自身现状的方法。
- **把封闭问题改写成开放问题**：不问「996 是福报吗」，问「996 是什么、怎么形成的、身处其中的我该怎么看」。
- **三层拆解**：① 概念本身（定义/行业/社会经济联系）② 对方动机（背景价值观、想达成什么、商业逻辑）③ 我的立场（对我的影响、如何改善）。
- **选书按作者**：优先「科学家+哲学家」合一的作者；长文/传统刊物通常比网文更有深度。
- **警惕焦虑闭环**：舒适 → 被激发焦虑（如「逆袭课程」）→ 寻求解决方案 → 消费决策。被制造的焦虑卖的是解药。
- ⚠️ 「网速提升一百万倍」是修辞不是数据，不要引用。
