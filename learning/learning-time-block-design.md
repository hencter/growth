# Learning Time-Block Design

## Overview

The failure mode is waiting for free time, which is why so much learning happens in January. The
counter-design: study is allocated like any other commitment — a fixed block, a defined granularity, and a
floor low enough that exhaustion cannot break the chain.

Three claims from the source material govern everything below:

1. **Quality over quantity** — 3 deep hours beat 6 fragmented ones.
2. **Specificity over intention** — "study Python at 8pm" fails; "8–9pm: chapter 3 loops, typed out" works.
3. **Continuity over intensity** — the chain matters more than any single session.

## Time granularity and the depth block

- **30-minute granularity.** Plan and measure in 30-minute units; it is small enough to fit real evenings
  and large enough to do work.
- **2 hours of deep work per day** as the target (from the growth-flywheel framing), rising to a 3-hour
  block on a good evening.
- **One protected block per day**, phone notifications off, is worth more than a dozen opportunistic slots.

### An evening template that fits a 9–6 job

| Time | Block | Type |
|------|-------|------|
| 19:30–20:15 | first push | depth begins |
| 20:15–20:30 | break / stretch | recovery is part of the design |
| 20:30–21:30 | main depth block, 25+5 pomodoro | the real work |
| 21:30–22:30 | light tasks, messages | low-load |
| 22:30–23:00 | plan tomorrow: three priorities + daily note | closing ritual |
| hard stop 23:00 | — | protects sleep (target > 7 hours) |

Weekend version: review and plan (60 min) → depth block 1 (90 min) → mandatory 2-hour break → depth block
2 (120 min) → light task → weekly review and next-week plan. One day of the weekend stays fully off.

## The micro-start floor

For exhausted or high-load days the floor is deliberately tiny: **5 minutes of study**, or **two pages**.
The purpose is not the output; it is that "I study daily" remains true, so restarting costs nothing. Plans
that only work at full capacity collapse at the first bad week and then stay collapsed.

## Anti-patterns

- **Waiting for free time** — there is no free time; there is allocated time.
- **Fragmented accumulation** — six 30-minute fragments treated as equivalent to three uninterrupted hours.
- **All-or-nothing** — no plan for the 30% of days that go badly.
- **Studying without a deadline** — every learning task gets a date, or it is a wish.
- **Growth at the expense of the job** — the schedule assumes work performance is protected; a plan that
  costs you the job is not a plan.

## 中文速览

- **不靠挤时间，靠分配时间**：先把学习这块「大石头」排进日程；以 **30 分钟**为时间颗粒度；每天保护 **2 小时深度工作**（好状态可到 3 小时晚块）；**3 小时深度 > 6 小时碎片**。
- **一个能落地的晚间模板**：19:30-20:15 推进 → 休息 15 分钟 → 20:30-21:30 主深度块（番茄钟 25+5）→ 21:30-22:30 轻度任务/沟通 → 22:30-23:00 计划明天三件事 + 日记 → **23:00 硬性收工**（睡眠 > 7 小时）。周末：回顾计划 → 深度块 1 → 强制 2 小时休息 → 深度块 2 → 周复盘；周末至少一天完全休息。
- **微量启动下限**：疲惫日允许「只学 5 分钟」「只读两页」——目的是让「我每天都在前进」这句话继续为真，重启成本为零。
- **计划要具体**：不是「晚上学 1 小时」，而是「今晚 8-9 点完成第 3 章并敲一遍代码」；每个学习任务都要有截止日期。
- ⚠️ 源方案自身算术不自洽：27 小时/周 × 13 周 ≈ 351 小时，与「90 天 310 小时」冲突——以 27 小时/周为排期口径，310 小时为目标口径，不要混用。
- 反面模式：等有空、把碎片时间等同于深度时间、没有为糟糕的日子留后手、学习任务无截止日期、以牺牲主业为代价。
