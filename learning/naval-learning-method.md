# Naval Learning Method

## Overview

Two complementary procedures, both attributed in the source material to Naval Ravikant and then
re-shaped into an executable workflow by the vault owner: **rapid research** for entering an unfamiliar
field quickly, and **non-linear reading** for building durable judgement. They serve different goals and
are not interchangeable.

| Goal | Use |
|------|-----|
| Enter a new field / solve a concrete problem now | Rapid research (four steps) |
| Build a knowledge system / improve judgement | Non-linear reading (three moves) |

The frame behind both: **judgement > effort, understanding > memorisation, depth > breadth, mental
models > isolated facts.**

## Rapid research — four steps

1. **Ask precisely.** Rewrite the question as open and personally owned. "Is Naval right?" becomes "Why
   does Naval phrase it this way, and what is the deeper logic?"
2. **Build a skeleton from parallel sources.** Take 3–5 classics (or equivalent primary sources) at
   once, extract only the structural claims, cross-check them against each other, and assemble a
   1-page skeleton before any deep read.
3. **Attack disagreements.** Where sources conflict, that is the live edge: compare, trace each claim
   to its evidence, and resolve or explicitly park it as unresolved.
4. **Close the loop.** Learn → internalise → output → take feedback → iterate. Output is the step that
   converts the skeleton into usable understanding (see [[output-based-retention]]).

## Non-linear reading — three moves

- **Read by structure, not by page order.** Directory → preface → conclusion → the two or three core
  chapters. The book's own argument map tells you where its real content is.
- **Run 3–5 related books in parallel, then deep-read the winners.** Parallel skimming is a filter, not
  a substitute for depth; breadth selects, depth pays.
- **Abandon without guilt; re-read the classics.** A book that has not earned its place is a
  time leak. Re-reading a classic is not regression — it is re-derivation at a higher level.

## Bias countermeasures

The source maps three biases to three concrete counter-moves:

| Bias | Counter-move |
|------|--------------|
| Confirmation bias | Actively search for disconfirming cases |
| Anchoring | Collect the same fact from several angles before fixing a reference point |
| Authority bias | Interrogate the *reasoning*, not the reputation |

## Anti-patterns

- Reading sequentially to completion before comparing anything.
- Building the skeleton from summaries instead of primary sources.
- Confusing input hours with capability; see [[learning-acceleration-loop]] phase 6.
- Collecting mental-model cards without applying any of them ([[mental-models-lattice]]).

## 中文速览

- **目标不同，方法不同**：快速进入新领域用「极速研究法」四步；构建知识体系用「独特阅读法」。
- **极速研究法**：精准提问（改写成开放式）→ 3-5 本经典并行提炼骨架 → 专攻各家冲突处 → 输出闭环。
- **独特阅读法**：按「目录→序言→结论→核心章节」读；3-5 本相关书并行筛选后深读；烂书立即放弃，经典反复重读。
- **三类偏差的对策**：确认偏差→主动找反例；锚定偏差→多角度取证；权威偏差→质疑推理而非名声。
- **总纲**：判断力>努力、理解>记忆、深度>广度、心智模型>具体知识。
- ⚠️ 源文件里的「学习金字塔百分比」与「7 年达到顶尖」属未证实断言，只取方向（动手与教人优于阅读），不引用具体数字。
