# Rapid Domain Entry Protocol

## Overview

Fields are not unstructured oceans. Every established field has a **canonical skeleton** — a small set of objects, questions, methods, tools, and live disputes — and that skeleton is extractable in hours rather than months, because other people have already paid the cost of organising it.

So the fast path is not "read a lot". It is: **extract the skeleton, acquire the language, reproduce one canonical result, find where the experts disagree, and publish an answer to your own question** — inside a fixed time box, with a stopping condition agreed before you start. This protocol is the executable version of [[learning-acceleration-loop|the learning loop]], specialised for *entering an unfamiliar field*.

Two design commitments make it fast rather than merely thorough:

1. **Every stage ends in an artifact, and every artifact has a gate.** No artifact = the stage did not happen.
2. **The marginal hour goes to reproduction and output, not to more input.** If you only have two hours, spend them on S3 + S5.

## Three depths (pick before you start)

| Depth | Budget | Deliverable | Use when |
|-------|-------:|-------------|----------|
| **T0 · 速通** | **3 h** | Decision-grade memo + 30-term glossary + field map | you need to judge, decide, or hold a competent conversation |
| **T1 · 上手** | **9.5 h over 7 days** (≈1.4 h/day) | T0 + **one reproduced canonical result** + 300–600-word explainer | you need to *do* something credible in the field |
| **T2 · 深入** | **29.5 h over ~4 weeks** | T1 + a real project reviewed against an outside standard + 2 reproductions + teaching a live audience | you need to be the person others ask |

Budgets are computed, not vibes: S0 0.5 + S1 2 + S2 1.5 + S3 2.5 + S4 1 + S5 1.5 + S6 0.5 = **9.5 h**, and the T2 figure adds ~20 h of project and deep-dive. Reproduction plus published output is **4 h of the 9.5 h (42%)** — that ratio is the whole point.
## What "genuinely learned" means (mastery criteria)

A certificate proves attendance at a process. These five criteria are what **真正学会** means operationally — the delivery standard at T2 and the review standard for every artifact at any depth:

| Criterion | Behavioural test | Fails when |
|-----------|------------------|------------|
| **Ask** | you can pose the field's *next* question yourself | you can only answer questions someone else framed |
| **Judge** | you can evaluate a new claim, design, or result with no authority to check against | you need a teacher or a citation to tell you whether it is any good |
| **Transfer** | you can apply it to a situation you have never seen | you can only redo the worked examples |
| **Teach** | you can teach it and survive the follow-up questions | your explanation collapses on the second "why?" |
| **Boundaries** | you can state precisely what you do *not* know yet | you cannot locate the edge of your own competence |

Rules: if any criterion is unmet, the honest word is **"familiar"**, not "learned" — say it out loud. Mastery is **domain-relative**: these criteria apply to the slice defined in S0, not to an entire civilisation of knowledge. And they are *behavioural tests, not feelings*: you demonstrate them, you never merely feel them.

## Assessment rule — read the answers, not the homework

Assessing a learner happens on **their own statements**: their questions, their judgements, their explanations, and their admissions of ignorance. Completing an artifact the mentor assigned is evidence of *compliance*, not of learning — useful, but never a substitute for the learner's own voice.

Consequences:

- An answer that only says "you decide" is **insufficient evidence**, and the honest report says so.
- Silence is reported as **no evidence**, never as "probably fine".
- A single self-report is low-sample evidence: label it as such.
- **Never introduce an uncalibrated term**: if the learner cannot yet explain a term in their own words, ask or teach it first — do not build a run on top of it.

**Insufficiency thresholds** — say these out loud instead of inventing progress:

| The learner says | Verdict |
|------------------|---------|
| "you pick one" / "whatever you think" | insufficient — the target is still the mentor's |
| a whole field name ("I want to learn AI") | insufficient — no object, no situation, no question |
| "I'm working on X, stuck at Y, I don't understand Z" | sufficient — object + situation + their own question |
## The seven stages

### S-1 · Diagnose the starting point (15 min) — artifact: the learner's own words

Before any target is set, establish where the learner actually **is** — with **open questions, never a menu**:

1. What is one thing you have been *trying* to understand and have not?
2. Where exactly does it stall — missing information, inability to judge, or not knowing how to act?
3. If you understood it a week from now, what would you do with it? (No use ⇒ it may be curiosity, not a learning target.)

Record the learner's **verbatim answers**, and refuse to design the run before you have them. A menu of options supplied by the mentor biases the choice — observed twice in one session: exam-flavoured options, and then a term the learner could not define.

> Gate: the learner has named the object **in their own sentence**. "You decide" is not an answer — it means the target is still yours, and the assessment would have no object.
### S0 · Define the target (30 min) — artifact: a one-paragraph contract

Write, in one paragraph:

- **Field** and the *specific slice* you are entering (fields are too big; "macroeconomics" is not a target, "reading a central-bank statement" is).
- **Deliverable**: a decision, a prototype, a conversation, an exam, a published piece.
- **Named reader** (even if it is you in six months).
- **Deadline** and the depth (T0/T1/T2).
- **Stopping condition**: what will be true when you stop — and what you deliberately will *not* learn this round.
- **One open question** — your Stage-5 output answers it ([[independent-thinking-against-information-overload|make it open, not yes/no]]).

> Gate: can you state the deliverable and its reader in one sentence? If not, stop here — the rest of the protocol would only produce well-organised drift.
> **Anti-pattern (learned 2026-10-05)**: never let the material at hand set the goal. A study plan already sitting in your vault, an exam calendar, or a course you already bought may *inform the path* — they must not *define the target*. Ask "what will I use this capability for?" first, then decide what to learn. (A concrete failure: a 12-week exam-prep plan in the vault turned "enter a field fast" into "prepare for an exam".)

### S1 · Map the field (2 h) — artifact: a one-page field map

Use five source *types* deliberately, because each reveals a different layer, and read **headings, abstracts, and terminology only**:

| Source type | What it gives you |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Graduate course syllabus / reading list | the canonical structure and sequence |
| Review or survey paper, handbook chapter | the map, the historical arc, the open problems |
| Standard textbook table of contents | the accepted partition of the subject |
| Encyclopedia / reference entry | vocabulary and stable definitions |
| Practitioner roadmap, job description, tool docs | what is actually used, what is obsolete |

Then force the map into five slots — this is the artifact:

```markdown
Objects      : what things does this field reason about?
Questions    : the 5–7 questions it exists to answer
Methods      : the standard procedures / models / formalisms
Tools        : what practitioners actually run, read, or build with
Disputes     : where credible sources contradict each other
Boundaries   : what this field explicitly does NOT cover
```

> Gate: can you state the field's five core questions **without notes**? If not, the map is a copy, not a model ([[naval-learning-method|skeleton first]]).

### S2 · Learn the language (1.5 h) — artifact: a 30–50 term glossary

Terms, one line each, **in your own words**, plus the confusion pair ("often confused with X"). Add 5–10 notation/formula items if the field is formal, with units and what each symbol means.

Rules:
- Every undefined term is a place where you will later nod along without understanding.
- Own words or it does not count — copying a definition is the failure mode that feels like progress.
- Keep the source id next to each term ([[source-credibility-and-observation-stance|grading]] applies from the first hour).

> Gate: 30 terms defined without looking, each in one sentence ([[review-and-recall-rhythm|retrieval, not re-reading]]).

### S3 · Reproduce one canonical result (2.5 h) — artifact: a run that matches the source

Find the field's smallest complete "hello world": recompute a textbook example, replicate a published figure within stated tolerance, run the standard pipeline on toy data, or build the minimal model and check its known limits.

You are not inventing; you are **proving the field's claims are executable by you**. When your numbers disagree with the source, that discrepancy is the highest-value learning event available — do not paper over it ([[investigation-before-judgement|investigate first]]).

> Gate: your output matches the source within a tolerance you stated *before* running, or you can explain precisely why it cannot.

### S4 · Locate the frontier (1 h) — artifact: 2–3 live disputes, with what would settle them

Look for the edges, because that is where remaining attention buys the most: "open problems" sections, survey conclusions, expert interviews, practitioner arguments in forums, version-change notes.

For each dispute record: **who disagrees · about what · what evidence would settle it · what I currently think**.

> Gate: you can name two disputes and the evidence that would resolve them. If everything looks settled, you have read only the textbook layer.

### S5 · Publish an answer (1.5 h) — artifact: a 300–600 word explainer

Answer your S0 question for the named reader, with sources, structured by [[sandwich-teaching-method|analogy → minimal definition → concrete demonstration]]. Deliver it — send it, publish it, or teach it aloud to one person.

> Gate: a named human read or heard it. Output is the only step that **cannot be faked** ([[output-based-retention|why output is not optional]]).

### S6 · Defend against decay (30 min now, 3 slots later) — artifact: vault entries + a calendar

- File in the vault: the field map, the glossary, and one atomic note capturing the reproduced result.
- Schedule retrieval at **day 3 / day 10 / day 30**: rebuild the map from memory, then diff against the original ([[review-and-recall-rhythm|reconstruct, don't re-read]]).
- Decide: **stop** (and archive with the stopping condition met), or **continue** with a next loop that raises depth (T1 → T2) — never continue by default.

## Where the marginal hour goes## Skill routing — load the skill, do not merely know it exists

Each stage has skills that already encode the operational discipline. **Load them with the `skill` tool before the stage starts**: a skill that is not loaded is not applied, and a stage run from memory is a stage run from habit.

| Stage | Load | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| S0 · target | `investigation-first` · `concentrate-forces` | survey the actual situation, then commit to one main target |
| S1 · map | `investigation-first` · `overall-planning` · `karlmarx-skill` | first-hand facts, balance competing goals, structural analysis |
| S2 · language | `cross-tag-method` · `obsidian-markdown` | classify terms; write notes that survive lint |
| S3 · reproduce | `practice-cognition` · `grill-me` | practice → understanding → practice loop; interrogate the plan before building |
| S4 · frontier | `contradiction-analysis` · `zoom-out` · `mass-line` | isolate the principal contradiction; raise abstraction; gather outside views |
| S5 · output | `criticism-self-criticism` · `minimalist-review` | honest self-review before delivery; cut what carries no weight |
| S6 · decay | `self-evolution` · `nova-kb` · `auto-commit` | distil the lesson, file and lint the vault, commit |
| blocked at any stage | `investigation-first` · `spark-prairie-fire` · `protracted-strategy` | unknown territory · start from almost nothing · long haul with no quick win |


If the budget shrinks, cut in this order — protect the bottom of the list:

| Priority | Stage | Why |
|---------:|-------|-----|
| 1 | S3 reproduction | the only proof of capability |
| 2 | S5 output | the only proof of understanding |
| 3 | S1 map | prevents organised ignorance |
| 4 | S4 disputes | raises judgement per hour |
| 5 | S2 glossary | accelerates everything else |
| 6 | S6 decay defence | cheap, but deferrable |
| 7 | S0 contract | non-negotiable at 30 minutes, not 3 hours |

## The 师友 split (who does what)

A mentor shortens the *search*, never the *understanding*. Explicit division:

| I do (fast, delegable) | You do (cannot be delegated) |
|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Source triage: pick the 5 source types, rank them, flag junk | Read into the field with your own eyes |
| Draft the field map and candidate dispute list | Correct it in your own words — the map is yours |
| Candidate glossary with sources and confusion pairs | Write your own one-line definitions |
| Reproduction recipe: exact steps, data, tolerance | Run it, debug it, explain the discrepancy |
| Adversarial review of your explainer before it ships | Answer the S0 question yourself, for a real reader |
| Keep the time box and call out drift | Decide to stop or continue |

Rules of engagement: I give **direct feedback against the gates**, name what is missing, and do not praise effort. If your artifact fails a gate, we repeat that stage rather than move on — silently skipping a gate is how "fast learning" becomes "fast forgetting".

## Interrupt rules

- **Sources disagree at S1** → record it in *Disputes*, do not resolve it yet; carrying the disagreement forward is correct behaviour, not failure.
- **Reproduction fails twice** → stop debugging the field and debug your setup: version, units, data range, hidden convention. Then ask me to re-derive the recipe from a second source.
- **Field turns out to be two fields** → stop, narrow to the slice that serves the S0 deliverable, restart S1 for that slice only.
- **Three hours in and lost** → the map is wrong, not you. Return to S1 and force the five slots; "lost" is almost always "no map".
- **Budget blown by 50%** → cut to the priority order above and ship a T0 artifact. A shipped T0 beats an unfinished T1.

## Failure modes

- **Linear reading** — starting at page 1 of the standard textbook. You cannot afford the field's own teaching sequence on a 10-hour budget.
- **Collector mode** — saving 40 sources and finishing none; input without an artifact.
- **Glossary by copy-paste** — definitions you cannot re-derive are decorations.
- **Skipping reproduction** — the comfortable path; it produces opinions, not capability.
- **Treating the textbook layer as the field** — no disputes means no map of the frontier.
- **No stopping condition** — "entering a field" without an exit becomes permanent study and zero capability.

## 中文速览

**一句话**：快速进入领域不是「读得多」，而是**按闸门生产四样东西**——领域地图、术语表、一次复现、一篇产出。

**三档预算（先选，再开始）**
- **T0 速通 3 小时**：决策级备忘 + 30 术语 + 领域地图（够你判断、决策、跟人对话）
- **T1 上手 9.5 小时 / 7 天（每天 1.36h）**：T0 + **复现一个经典结果** + 300-600 字讲解
- **T2 深入 29.5 小时 / 4 周**：T1 + 真实项目（有外部标准验收）+ 2 次复现 + 讲给活人听

**七阶段（每阶段必须交东西，交不出就是没做）**

| 阶段 | 产出 | 闸门（过不了就重做本阶段） |
|---|---|---|
| S0 定义目标 0.5h | 一段「合同」：领域切片 / 交付物 / 给谁看 / 死线 / **止损条件** / 一个开放式问题 | 能用一句话说清交付物与读者 |
| S1 画地图 2h | **一页五格**：对象 / 核心问题 / 方法 / 工具 / **分歧**（+ 边界） | 不看笔记能说出 5 个核心问题 |
| S2 学语言 1.5h | 30-50 条术语，**每条用自己的话** + 易混对照 | 不看资料能解释 30 条 |
| S3 复现 2.5h | 跑通该领域最小「hello world」，与源结果对账 | 结果落在**事先声明的容差**内，或能解释为何不能 |
| S4 找前沿 1h | 2-3 个**真实分歧**：谁反对谁 / 争什么 / 什么证据能定论 | 说得清两个分歧与定论条件 |
| S5 产出 1.5h | 300-600 字，回答你的 S0 问题，讲给具名读者 | **有具名的人读过或听过** |
| S6 抗遗忘 0.5h | 地图/术语/复现结论入库 + 第 3/10/30 天检索式复习 | 复习是「合上资料重建」而不是重读 |

**预算被压缩时按这个顺序砍**：复现 > 产出 > 地图 > 分歧 > 术语 > 抗遗忘（S0 的 30 分钟不可省）。

**师友分工（我做搜索，你做理解）**
- 我做：选源与排序、地图与分歧候选、术语候选、复现配方（步骤/数据/容差）、对你的讲解做对抗性评审、盯时间盒
- 你必须做：用自己的话写定义、亲手跑复现、自己回答 S0 问题、自己决定停还是继续

**止损与自适应**：源互相矛盾 → 记进分歧、不急着解决；复现两次失败 → 先查版本/单位/数据范围/隐含约定；发现是「两个领域」 → 收窄到 S0 需要的那一片重跑 S1；3 小时还发懵 → 一定是地图错了，回 S1；预算超 50% → 砍成 T0 交活。**交出一个 T0，胜过烂尾的 T1。**

**反模式**：从教科书第 1 页线性读、收藏 40 个源全没读完、术语靠复制粘贴、跳过复现、把教材层当成整个领域、没有止损条件。
