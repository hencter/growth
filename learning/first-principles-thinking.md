# First Principles Thinking

## Overview

Aristotle's "first principle" is the basic proposition of a system that cannot be deleted or violated
without destroying the system. Reasoning by analogy copies what already exists (fast, incremental);
reasoning from first principles rebuilds from verifiable facts (slow, occasionally disruptive). Use
analogy by default; switch to first principles when the existing solution is the problem.

## The chain

1. **Identify the assumptions** currently carrying the problem.
2. **Decompose** the problem into its constituent parts.
3. **Return to the source** — the physics, the arithmetic, the actual constraint.
4. **Rebuild from zero** — construct the solution out of the base facts only.
5. **Verify and iterate** — test the reconstruction against reality.

Musk's three-move compression of the same chain: define the assumption → decompose to fundamentals →
create a new solution from scratch.

## The Socratic self-check

Run these before accepting any inherited constraint:

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Why do I think this? | Surface the belief |
| Where did it come from? | Trace the source (often hearsay) |
| Is it actually true? | Separate fact from convention |
| What if the opposite were true? | Break anchoring |
| What evidence exists? | Demand support |
| What if I am wrong — what then? | Price the downside |

This check is the same instrument as [[investigation-before-judgement]]: the point is to find the fact
floor before building anything on top of a guess.

## Worked reductions (as reported in the source)

| Case | Reduction | Result claimed |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Rocket launch cost | Vehicle is aluminium/titanium/copper/carbon fibre → materials ≈ **2%** of price → the other **98%** is process | In-house build → launch cost ≈ **10%** of prior |
| EV battery | Market price quoted at **$250–600/kWh**; raw-material floor ≈ **$80/kWh** | In-house → below **$100/kWh** |

> ⚠️ These figures come from the source note as second-hand industry anecdotes; they are **not**
> independently verified here. Use them as illustrations of the method, not as data to cite. The
> transferable content is the *move*: locate the 2% that is physical and the 98% that is process.

## Applying it to learning itself

- "I must read the whole book" → what is actually required? *A skeleton of the field's claims.*
  → build the skeleton from 3–5 sources ([[naval-learning-method]]).
- "This field takes years" → what does the first week actually need? *The question, the base facts,
  one verified experiment* ([[learning-acceleration-loop]]).
- "I learn slowly" → is the constraint input speed, or the absence of an output phase
  ([[output-based-retention]])?

## Anti-patterns

- **First-principles theatre** — re-deriving everything for decisions where analogy is adequate. This
  is expensive and is why the mode is situational.
- **Stopping at decomposition** — listing parts without returning to a verifiable floor; the rebuild
  step is what makes the method work.
- **Quoting the anecdotes as evidence** — see the warning above.

## 中文速览

- **定义**：第一性原理 = 系统中不可被违背或删除的最基本命题；从它往上推导，而不是照抄已有做法。
- **链条**：识别假设 → 拆解问题 → 回归本源 → 从零构建 → 验证迭代（马斯克三步版：定义假设 → 拆到基础原理 → 从零造新方案）。
- **苏格拉底六问自查**：我为什么这样想？来源是什么？真的对吗？反过来会怎样？有什么证据？我错了会怎样？
- **拆解范例（源文件二手转述，未独立核实）**：火箭材料成本约 2%、其余 98% 是制造过程 → 自研后发射成本降至 10%；电池市场价 250-600 美元/千瓦时、材料底价约 80 美元/千瓦时 → 自研后低于 100 美元/千瓦时。**只学「2% 是物理、98% 是流程」这个动作，别引用这些数字。**
- **用在学习上**：「必须读完一本书」→ 真正需要的是这个领域主张的骨架；「学得慢」→ 先问瓶颈是输入速度还是缺少输出环节。
